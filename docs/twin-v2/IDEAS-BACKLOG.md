# Ideas backlog

A large pool of further ideas for walk mode, free roam and story mode, gathered at the owner's request (2026-10-10). **The owner has asked for all of it to be built, one item at a time, in the order set out under "Build order" at the end of this file.** A star put in front of a line by the owner moves it earlier.

Effort tags are rough: **S** one session or less, **M** two to three sessions, **L** a multi-session system that needs its own design pass first. Items marked **check** need a licence, source or feasibility check before building.

## 1. Story: more threads for "Unang Liwanag"

- (M) **Chronicles:** short playable flashbacks as someone else: the first surveyor alone on the ridge in 2022; the guard on the typhoon night; Kuya Ben at the tunnel breakthrough; Aling Rosing's first day cooking for forty with one burner.
- (M) **The old adit:** a sealed tunnel from a logging or prospecting venture decades ago, found during excavation: tools, a ledger, a name scratched in rock, and why it was abandoned. Feeds the night story.
- (M) **The treasure rumour:** talk of wartime gold buried in these hills (a common Filipino legend). Diggers appear at night, a map changes hands, and the truth is smaller and sadder than gold.
- (M) **Poachers and timber cutters:** human trouble in the forest at night; snares to find and remove, a chainsaw heard far off, a choice between reporting a poor man and letting it go. Moves Kalikasan.
- (S) **The padugo:** the old builders' custom of an offering before work begins on a structure. Whether each front had one, who insisted and who laughed, matters to the Diwa standing later.
- (S) **Pamahiin (site superstitions) as a quiet system:** do not whistle in the tunnel, do not point at a mound, say "tabi-tabi po", do not go straight home after a wake. Observing or ignoring them shifts Diwa and how the believers on the crew treat you.
- (M) **The barangay:** a fictional village below the gate with a chapel, a store, a school and a basketball court: a wedding, a wake, a fiesta, a flood night when the crew helps evacuate, children who visit the site on a school day.
- (M) **Family far away:** your character's family in the province, present through calls, letters and photos: a child's first day of school, a sick parent, the wish to go home. A "home" goal you save for.
- (M) **A romance** with an invented character only, slow and optional, told mostly in mornings.
- (S) **Pay delay week:** tension in the camp when wages are late; tempers, a spokesperson, a fair resolution. Invented figures only.
- (S) **The inspector's visit** and **the journalist's visit:** a day of everyone on best behaviour, and what you choose to show.
- (M) **Rival crew:** the weir camp against the powerhouse camp in work targets and in the basketball league, building to a final.
- (S) **Mentor's last day:** a veteran retires; his tools pass to you.
- (M) **Several endings** from the three standings, and a **new game plus** that lets you begin again in a different career with what you learned.
- (S) **Dreams:** on nights you sleep badly or after a case, short strange playable dreams that hint at what is unresolved.
- (L) **A second campaign** as the project manager from day one, where the missions are decisions and the site is the board.

## 2. Night: more presences, more ways to meet them

Each written from documented folklore, sourced in `data/story/folklore.json`.

- (S) **Kumakatok:** three knocks on a door at night. Uses the door system: you decide whether to open.
- (S) **Batibat:** a spirit of a felled tree that follows its timber; a post in the barracks cut from the wrong tree, and the man who sleeps beneath it.
- (S) **Sigbin** shadowing the animals at the camp edge; **manananggal** rumour from the barangay; **pugot** on the upper trail; a **black lady** at the powerhouse stair; a **ghost truck** whose headlights climb the road and never arrive.
- (M) **Totoy's sight:** playing short sections as the boy who sees what others cannot.
- (S) **Lolo Ambo's readings:** candle-wax divination to point a case in a direction; herbs gathered by day for a remedy.
- (S) **Fireside stories:** the evening circle tells tales; listening to one unlocks the case it is about.
- (S) **Night photography:** some things show only in a photograph; an album of the unexplained.
- (S) **The radio at 3 a.m.:** static, a voice between stations, a channel nobody uses.
- (M) **Safe rooms and long nights:** nights you must simply get through until dawn in one place with what light you have.
- (S) **The hours matter:** dusk, midnight and three in the morning each change what can happen; the hour before dawn is the safest and the loneliest.
- (S) **Doubt:** every case offers a natural explanation as well; the journal keeps both, and the game never tells you which is true until the end.

## 3. Living: more of a life

- (M) **Hair and beard grow;** a haircut and a shave from the camp barber; a tan that deepens through the dry months.
- (M) **Clothes get worn and need washing;** a laundry routine; mending; new boots when the old ones split.
- (M) **Health:** fever, a cut that needs cleaning, an upset stomach from bad food, leeches in the wet months, mosquitoes and a net over the bunk, the clinic's medicines, vaccination day.
- (S) **Sleep quality** from noise, heat, a full stomach, nerves, the fan working or not.
- (S) **Drink has effects:** join the tagay and your walk sways, sight softens, tongue loosens, and the morning after is heavy. Turn up to work like that and the foreman sends you back.
- (M) **Money with meaning:** remittance home each pay-day, savings toward a motorcycle, a debt to a friend (utang) and the awkwardness of it, lending to others and whether you get it back.
- (M) **A place of your own:** bunk to shared room to a staff-house room as you rise; later a small hut you build yourself by the garden.
- (M) **Your motorcycle:** buy it second-hand, fix it with the mechanic, paint it, ride it to the barangay.
- (M) **Garden and animals:** your own plot through the seasons; chickens for eggs; a goat; selling the surplus to the kitchen.
- (M) **Cooking:** a recipe book of Filipino dishes learned from Aling Rosing, cooked by steps at the outdoor kitchen, each with an effect on needs and on whoever you share it with.
- (M) **Fishing as a hobby:** species by pool, season, weather and hour; bait; a record book.
- (S) **Foraging:** wild fruit, edible ferns and mushrooms; some are a mistake.
- (M) **Crafting from site scrap** with the carpenters: a stool, a shelf, a fishing rod, a bird feeder, a lantern.
- (S) **Side work:** cut hair, sell phone load, fix radios, wash motorcycles.
- (S) **Birthdays, yours included,** and a year-end summary of your life on site.

## 4. Things to do and places to go

- (M) **Trails:** to a waterfall, a ridge-top lookout, a cave with bats, a hot afternoon swimming hole, the old survey line. Camping overnight with a fire.
- (M) **The river:** a bamboo raft, crossing by rope when it is high, diving for a dropped tool, stone stacking.
- (S) **A cableway bucket** across the river at the weir, if the owner confirms one exists; otherwise a rope bridge on the trail.
- (S) **Time trials:** the gate to the powerhouse by motorcycle or pickup, dry and wet records.
- (S) **Drone courses** through the works.
- (M) **Sports and games:** a basketball league with a season and standings; volleyball; sepak takraw; chess and dama ladders; billiards; darts; arm wrestling; tong-its and other card games for in-game pesos.
- (M) **Fiesta games:** tug of war, sack race, the greased pole, a singing contest, a dance number at the Christmas party you can rehearse for.
- (S) **Videoke with scoring,** using only original or public-domain songs (check).
- (S) **A camp band:** guitar, a box drum and whoever sings.
- (S) **Photography contests** on the notice board; bird and wildlife lists; a postcard maker.
- (S) **Stargazing** with named constellations on clear moonless nights.
- (S) **Hidden things:** time capsules, initials in concrete, a cat with kittens in the warehouse, the best mango tree.

## 5. The site as a living system

- (M) **Materials that run out:** cement, fuel, rebar and rock bolts are stocked, used and re-ordered; a late truck stalls a pour and becomes a mission (the monthly reviews are full of exactly this).
- (M) **Breakdowns:** machines wear, fail and wait for parts; the mechanic's queue shapes the day.
- (M) **Emergencies with drills behind them:** an earthquake and the inspection after; a grass fire in the dry season; rockfall on the road; water coming into the tunnel and the walk out; a gas alarm; a man overcome by heat. Invented characters only; told seriously.
- (M) **People have lives:** friendships and feuds among the crew, gossip that travels, promotions, arrivals and send-offs, a wedding, a new baby's photo passed round, someone who quits.
- (S) **A notice board and a camp radio** that report the site's real state, the weather, the league, birthdays.
- (S) **Long memory:** people remember what you did months ago and mention it.
- (M) **The forest changes with how the site is run:** more or fewer animals at the edge, a recovering slope where trees were planted, a stream running clear or brown.

## 6. Movement and animation sets

- (M) **Emotion on faces and bodies:** joy, pride, worry, fear, anger, grief, embarrassment, boredom, each as a face set and a way of standing and walking.
- (M) **Hurt and tired:** limp, favour an arm, hold the ribs, wipe a bloody nose, sit down heavily, be helped to walk, be carried, lie on the clinic bed, recover over days.
- (M) **Knock-downs with physics:** a body that falls believably from a blow or a slip and gets up in several ways (check: needs a light physics layer).
- (S) **Fear:** flinch, freeze, back away, run looking over the shoulder, hide, peer round a corner, hands shaking a torch.
- (S) **Body states:** tipsy sway, shiver, yawn and stumble from tiredness, cough in dust, sneeze, scratch a bite, stretch a sore back.
- (M) **Water:** wade, swim, tread, dive, climb out dripping.
- (M) **Climbing:** rope, rock, tree, scaffold, a truck's side.
- (S) **Carrying variety:** on the head, on a pole between two, in a sack on the back, a child on the shoulders at the fiesta.
- (S) **Prayer, mourning, celebration:** sign of the cross, kneeling, lighting a candle, a cheer, lifting a team-mate, dancing.
- (S) **Sleep:** positions, turning, waking with a start.
- (M) **Sport sets:** full basketball moves, volleyball, sepak takraw kicks.
- (S) **Hands:** counting money, shuffling and dealing cards, striking a match, peeling fruit, tying a knot, texting.
- (M) **Cloth and hair that move:** shirt tails, raincoats, towels, long hair, wet hair.

## 7. Effects

- (M) **Fire:** cooking fires, a campfire you build and feed, a welding spark that catches dry grass, a grass fire that spreads with wind and is beaten out.
- (M) **Breakable things:** glass, hollow blocks, plywood, crates, a windscreen.
- (M) **Vehicle wear:** dents, a cracked lamp, a flat tyre, steam from an overheated engine, fuel that runs out, working gauges, wipers, a cab radio.
- (S) **Water on the lens** in rain and after a splash; underwater sight and sound.
- (S) **Breath in cold air; sweat that drips; tears.**
- (S) **Lightning that strikes a tree;** a branch that falls in wind.
- (S) **Night:** eyeshine, a torch beam in mist, a candle's small light, moon shadows, fog you can get lost in.
- (S) **Blood** as section 4 of the story file describes: on skin, cloth and ground, drying and washing off.
- (S) **Controller rumble and phone vibration** tuned per event.

## 8. Play that helps the company

- (M) **New-hire orientation:** learn the site layout, the emergency routes and muster points by walking them.
- (S) **Emergency drill rehearsal** with a timed head count.
- (M) **"How the plant works":** follow a drop of water from the weir to the grid, with each structure explaining itself.
- (S) **Client tour script** in presentation mode that reuses the story's best views.

## How this list is used

1. The owner stars the lines he cares about most.
2. A pack session takes a handful that fit together, checks any marked **check**, writes the detail (missions, lines, clips, data), builds and tests them.
3. Items tagged **L** get a design sub-phase of their own first.
4. Anything that touches real colleagues follows the rules in `P16-story-mode.md` sections 2 and 4.
