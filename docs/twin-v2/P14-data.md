# P14. Real data, progress, links

Two sessions. Make it a twin in substance: what it shows about the project is real, and what is not real says so.

The page is public (`proxy.ts` lists `/digital-twin`). Anything fetched here reaches signed-out visitors, so every server response is a deliberately chosen public shape.

## P14a. Real records and honest labels

**Needs.** P13a.

**Read first.** `prisma/schema.prisma` models `PlantEquipment`, `EquipmentMaintenanceLog`, `SiteLocation`, `Milestone`, `ProgressSnapshot`, `CodMilestone`, `AccomplishmentReport`, `Project`; `app/(dashboard)/dashboard/sitemap/actions.ts` (`getEquipmentByLocation`); `lib/services/projectProfileService.ts` (how other pages read project data); `lib/weather/pagasa.ts`; v1 `PlantScene.tsx` `DEFAULT_EQUIPMENTS` and `AlertsFeedPanel` (what must not be repeated); the Next docs for route handlers and caching in `node_modules/next/dist/docs/`; the saved note that the app must use the Supabase transaction pooler.

**Steps.**
1. `app/api/twin/[projectId]/public/route.ts`: one read-only endpoint returning a public shape: equipment (tag, name, category, status, condition, zone), counts by status, milestones (title, planned and actual dates, status), latest progress percentage with its date, and the list of structures with their current stage if derivable. No user ids, no internal notes, no documents. Cached for a few minutes. Add it to the public matcher only if it is not already covered.
2. The twin stops calling `getEquipmentByLocation` directly; it reads the endpoint.
3. Equipment: picks in the scene bind to records by `equipmentTag`. An item with a record shows its status colour and details in the inspector; an item without a record shows its tag and "No record in Nexus". There is no built-in fallback list.
4. Status panel replacing v1's alerts feed: maintenance and condition items from real logs, with their real timestamps; if there are none, "No open items".
5. Remove or source every number from the inventory's "numbers shown" table. Plant output: show only if a real source exists; otherwise the energy-flow layer is labelled "Illustration".
6. Label rules, enforced in one place (`ui/Provenance.tsx`): `Live` (from a record or feed, with its time), `Recorded` (from a record, with its date), `Simulated` (generated by the twin: people's activity, weather override, clock override), `Illustration` (static depiction). Every figure and status chip renders through it.
7. Weather: PAGASA feed as in v1; the top-bar pill reflects real versus override.
8. Honour `?project=<id>`: Tumauini's id loads the twin; any other id shows a clear page ("No 3D model for this project yet") with a link back to that project's profile. Fix the `dynamicMode` export in the route file.
9. Links out: person to the People panel entry; equipment to its Nexus page; facility to the site-map location. Links to pages that need sign-in are marked with a lock.
10. Links in: update `lib/services/projectProfileService.ts` consumers only if needed so "Open Digital Twin" lands on `?v=2&project=<id>` after cut-over (not before P15).

**Pass when.** With the database empty of equipment, the twin shows no invented equipment and says so; a grep of `components/twin/` finds no hard-coded counts, times or ratings outside `data/` files that cite a source; signed-out fetch of the endpoint returns only the public shape.

## P14b. Construction progress and second-project readiness

**Read first.** `Milestone`, `CodMilestone`, `ProgressSnapshot`, `AccomplishmentReport` models and where the dashboard writes them; `CONTRACTS.md` 4.2 (`stage`); P05f.

**What exists.** The owner has approved showing real physical progress and completion dates publicly. `data/progress.json` holds monthly actual (and planned) percentages per work front from October 2025, and overall figures before that where readable; `data/history.json` holds dated construction events per structure from April 2022 (surveys, access roads from September 2022, main camp from July 2023, Tunnel 1 outlet portal from November 2023, river diversion from March 2024, powerhouse excavation from December 2024, surge tank from February 2025, powerhouse structure from September 2025, lining and electromechanical works in 2026). Both were compiled in P00a from the monthly reviews and cite their slides. New months are added by re-running the P00a extraction on a newly downloaded deck, or from Nexus records once the dashboard holds them.

**Steps.**
0. Show progress: on the scheme map and in each structure's inspector, the work front's actual percentage with its "as of" month, the planned percentage, and the revised completion date. Label them `Recorded`. Do not show schedule variance in days, manpower shortfalls, issues lists or any cost figure.
1. `data/structures.json`: each structure id, its stage sequence, and a mapping rule from real records to a stage (for example, a milestone titled for powerhouse concrete being complete sets `poured`). Rules are explicit and reviewed with the owner; no fuzzy guessing at run time.
2. Timeline control in the Time panel: a second scrubber over project dates. Dragging it sets each structure's stage as of that date from recorded milestones and snapshots. Dates with records show a marker; stretches without records are drawn hatched and labelled "No recorded data: illustrative sequence".
3. The workforce follows stage: gangs and stations exist only where the stage needs them (P09 already filters by `stage`).
4. "Today" is the default and reflects the latest records.
5. Second-project readiness, without building a second site: move everything Tumauini-specific behind a project manifest (`components/twin/data/projects/tumauini/manifest.json` listing its zones, people, programme, routes, species, tours, coordinates) and make `TwinApp` load by manifest. Write `docs/twin-v2/ADDING-A-PROJECT.md` describing what a second project would need.
6. Public summary card on the overview: project name, contractor, a progress figure with its recorded date, and next milestone, all from the endpoint, each with provenance.

**Pass when.** Scrubbing project dates changes structures and crews coherently; every stage shown for "today" traces to a record or is labelled illustrative; the app boots from the manifest with no Tumauini constants left in engine, sim or UI code.
